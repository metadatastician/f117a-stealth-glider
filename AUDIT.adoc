= Audit — F117A Stealth Glider
:toc: macro
:toclevels: 2

What is *verified*, what is merely *argued*, and what is *not claimed*.
This document exists so that nobody has to infer the difference from a green tick.

toc::[]

== The headline

The line-of-sight mechanic is proven, and the level now earns its premise:
claim C6 — the falsifier for the entire premise — is *GREEN*, measured, and
gates the build.

It was red from this repository's creation until the level geometry was made
to deserve otherwise, and the assertions were *tightened* before the level was
made to pass them (see the falsifier section below).

And the game now exists: a chase-camera 3D layer over the same 2D Conway
world, a tactical map, a single self-contained playable bundle. Every claim
number once reserved for it — C4 (corridor), C7 (renderer inertness), C9
(3D rule search), C10 (bundle reproducibility) — landed with it and gates.

== Verified

These are discharged by a script in `src/`, runnable with plain `node`, that can
actually fail. Where a check could plausibly be vacuous, it ships with a canary
demonstrating it failing on purpose.

[cols="1,4"]
|===
| C1 | `src/kernel/engine.mjs` is byte-identical to f19@9effa596, and `src/mission.mjs` is that file plus five fenced blocks. Asserted as git blob ids, so it is cryptographic and offline. Canary: a one-token edit outside a fence breaks it.
| C2 | Supercover line of sight does not leak through a diagonal wall — 0 of 4489 cross-wall pairs. Canary: naive Bresenham leaks 1009 of 4489 through the same wall.
| C3 | A radar does not occlude itself. Canary: without the footprint exemption it blinds itself 5/5.
| C4 | The nominal corridor's exposure is phase-shaped and pinned: 31 always / 42 never / 8 phase-dependent of 81 cells, 36 with a consecutive run >= LOCK. Structural gates plus exact declared counts. Also: the whole-map shadow is a function of substrate phase alone (75 repeats, 0 mismatches) — the property the renderer's phase cache rests on.
| C5 | The witness route LANDS at t=469, trace 0, peak exposure 7 of 8 — and was painted at some point, so it is not trivially safe.
| C6 | The falsifier: 14 of 14 off-phase launches of the witness are PAINTED, the on-phase flight lands, and the exhaustive search finds no route that stays below exposure 7. The level is solvable only by reading phase.

== The falsifier: red, then earned

=== C6

Fly the *same* route from every launch phase. For the level to be a phase puzzle,
most off-phase launches must fail, and fail specifically as `PAINTED`.

*Measured now: 14 of 14 off-phase launches are shot down as PAINTED; the
on-phase flight lands with trace 0; and the exhaustive search finds no route
anywhere in the state space that stays below exposure 7.*

The path from 0/30 to that measurement is the audit trail, because each step
closed a distinct way the substrate could have been scenery:

* *The wall had to be a wall.* The first chokepoint spanned ~50 cells of a line
  whose in-bounds length is ~106, leaving open map beyond both ends. The route
  flew round it, so the shutter was guarding a path nobody had to take. (0/30
  with peak exposure constant at 7 — the automaton contributed nothing.)
* *Tangency beats coverage.* A 72-position sweep of the shutter shows a
  pentadecathlon placed squarely on the gate-to-gap sight line masking the gap at
  every phase — 15/15 land, peak flat at 7..7. Grazing the ray at [68,68] leaves
  only its extended phases occluding: 4/14.
* *The sensor had to sit where the route is exposed.* An exposure diagnostic
  showed every launch phase peaking at 7 or 8 of `LOCK` 8 — one dwell generation
  decides every flight. A 507-point placement sweep moved the gate sensor from
  (76,74) to (79,73): 14/14 on the witness.
* *The witness was not allowed to be the loophole.* The witness-scoped
  assertions cannot see a different route that lands at every phase, and one
  existed, measured: a zigzag hugging the gate's range circle, dipping into the
  disc for 3–4 generations and draining the dwell counter between dips — peak
  exposure 4, landing at all 15 phases. C6 gained a fourth, solver-relative
  assertion (the same search that proposed the witness must find *no* route
  below exposure `LOCK-1`), and sealing it took gate range 23 plus a second
  pentadecathlon at (66,79) across the gate-to-egress rays — a one-cell optimum:
  (67,79) measures 1/14, (66,80) 4/14, (66,79) 14/14 with the escape gone.

The assertions were tightened before the level was made to pass them: the
majority clause previously accepted `landed <= n/2` (satisfiable with 4 of 14
painted), and there was no no-escape clause at all. The searches that found the
geometry are `design/place.mjs` (placement, not scalars — the earlier
conclusion that the level could not be made phase-critical had swept only
`radarRange`, `LOCK` and spacing, and never moved a sensor) and its pipeline
re-used over sensor position, range, and the second shutter.

Of the levers the red ledger listed as untried, the second gate *sensor* was
swept — 2,016 configurations — and measured dead: every candidate collided,
broke periodicity, or sealed the map at all phases. The second *shutter* on the
existing sensor is what shipped.

== Argued, not verified

* *That the chosen mechanic is the right translation of F-117A.* Terrain masking
  and radar cross-section were that aircraft's actual defence, and moving cover is
  the natural cellular-automaton reading of it. This is a design argument. It is
  not a measurement.
* *That "one exposed cell paints the aircraft" is the right rule.* Requiring all
  five cells to be exposed would make the glider's own spread act as free cover.
  The conservative rule is chosen deliberately and stated in `src/radar.mjs`; it is
  not derived from anything.
* *That LOCK = 8 is the right dwell.* Chosen so a one-generation flicker is
  survivable and sustained exposure is not. `design/sweep.mjs` shows 5 and 6 make
  the current level unsolvable; that bounds it, but does not justify 8 specifically.
* *That the 3D form should have been rejected.* The measurement is now a
  permanent gating test (C9: 0 spaceships from 180 seeded soups per rule), but
  "no glider ecology" remains an INFERENCE from it — a directed search finds
  Bays's gliders where soups do not. What C9 verifies is exactly the soup
  statement; the design conclusion drawn from it lives here, one shelf down.

== NOT claimed

* *The game is not claimed to be balanced or finished as a GAME.* It is
  playable — chase camera, tactical map, HUD, one committed bundle — and its
  presentation layer is proven inert (C7), but nothing measures whether the
  difficulty curve, camera feel or readability are any good. Those are design
  judgements; `just play` is the probe.
* *The level is not claimed to be good.* C6 makes it phase-critical — a
  measured statement about geometry, not about whether it is any fun to play.
* *Performance is measured for the one hot path, and not claimed beyond it.*
  `shadowMap` is O(W·H·sensors·ray) and *measures 23.5 ms* on this level —
  more than a 16.7 ms frame budget, and about 70% of a core at 30 generations
  per second. It is therefore memoised by substrate phase: the level is
  periodic from generation 0 with period 15, so only fifteen distinct shadow
  maps exist while the world is pure substrate, and the per-tick cost becomes
  an array index (0.0002 ms). C4 asserts the property the cache rests on, and
  a contractile probe fails if the call moves into the frame loop. Nothing
  measures end-to-end frame rate, draw-list cost, or behaviour on low-end
  hardware.
* *No claim that the solver is a second implementation of the engine.* It is an
  approximation that orders the collision and radar tests differently for speed.
  It may only propose; `verify-witness.mjs` flying the route through the real
  `missionStep()` is what makes C5 a claim. If the two ever disagree, the solver
  is wrong.
* *No claim about `spike/`.* Those two files are provenance, landed verbatim.
  They are not built, not linted, not imported, and their seven recorded defects
  are not fixed in place.
* *No security claim beyond the obvious.* This is a dependency-free static page
  with no network access and no user data.

== Deviations

* *(closed)* C6 ran red under `continue-on-error` from creation until the level
  geometry earned it; it now gates the build from inside the `ledger` job.
* *`spike/` is exempt from line-ending normalisation* (`.gitattributes`), because
  `simulation.html` carries 162 CRLF terminators and `text=auto eol=lf` would
  rewrite them, breaking the published SHA-256 digests and the byte-identical
  claim with them.
