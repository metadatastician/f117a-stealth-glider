= Audit — F117A Stealth Glider
:toc: macro
:toclevels: 2

What is *verified*, what is merely *argued*, and what is *not claimed*.
This document exists so that nobody has to infer the difference from a green tick.

toc::[]

== The headline

The line-of-sight mechanic is proven. *The level is not finished.*

Claim C6 — the falsifier for the entire premise — is *RED*, and that is a
measured result rather than an unstarted task. It is reported by CI on every
push and does not gate the build while it is known-red, because a red gate that
everyone learns to step over is worse than an honest statement of what is
unfinished.

== Verified

These are discharged by a script in `src/`, runnable with plain `node`, that can
actually fail. Where a check could plausibly be vacuous, it ships with a canary
demonstrating it failing on purpose.

[cols="1,4"]
|===
| C1 | `src/kernel/engine.mjs` is byte-identical to f19@9effa596, and `src/mission.mjs` is that file plus five fenced blocks. Asserted as git blob ids, so it is cryptographic and offline. Canary: a one-token edit outside a fence breaks it.
| C2 | Supercover line of sight does not leak through a diagonal wall — 0 of 4489 cross-wall pairs. Canary: naive Bresenham leaks 1009 of 4489 through the same wall.
| C3 | A radar does not occlude itself. Canary: without the footprint exemption it blinds itself 5/5.
| C5 | The witness route LANDS at t=430, trace 0, peak exposure 7 of 8 — and was painted at some point, so it is not trivially safe.
| C8 | Determinism, checked twice over: no non-determinism primitive in the four simulation modules, and two full runs hash identically across every generation.
| C11 | The substrate is periodic from generation 0 — transient 0, period 30 as declared, population 261..375.
| C12 | No two stamped patterns are within Chebyshev 3; everything is inside the border; the hangar zone is inside the grid; spawn is on a clean landing lane.
|===

== Red, and known

=== C6 — the falsifier

Fly the *same* route from every launch phase. For the level to be a phase puzzle,
most off-phase launches must fail, and fail specifically as `PAINTED`.

*Measured: 3 of 14 off-phase launches are shot down as PAINTED; peak exposure
varies 7..8 across phases.*

Two of C6's three assertions now pass — failures are dominated by radar rather
than by terrain, and at least one launch is locked. The third does not: a
*majority* must fail, and 3/14 is not a majority. So the radar is load-bearing
but not yet decisive.

*This is a large improvement on the first design*, and the difference is
instructive. That design flanked an open corridor with shutter banks and
measured *0 of 30* phases failing, with peak exposure constant at 7 — the solver
simply routed around the banks through permanent cover. Cover beside a route you
need not take is scenery.

The fix was a *chokepoint*: a wall across the corridor with one gap, a
short-range gate sensor watching it, and a p15 pentadecathlon shuttering the
sight line to the gap. What remains is to make the gap the *only* survivable
option — currently 11 of 14 phases still find a way through.

The search that found this is `design/place.mjs`, which sweeps *placement*
rather than scalars. The earlier conclusion that the level could not be made
phase-critical was unearned: it swept `radarRange`, `LOCK` and pattern spacing
and never moved a sensor. Geometry problems are not fixed by tuning numbers.

== Argued, not verified

* *That the chosen mechanic is the right translation of F-117A.* Terrain masking
  and radar cross-section were that aircraft's actual defence, and moving cover is
  the natural cellular-automaton reading of it. This is a design argument. It is
  not a measurement.
* *That "one exposed cell paints the aircraft" is the right rule.* Requiring all
  five cells to be exposed would make the glider's own spread act as free cover.
  The conservative rule is chosen deliberately and stated in `src/radar.mjs`; it is
  not derived from anything.
* *That LOCK = 8 is the right dwell.* Chosen so a one-generation flicker is
  survivable and sustained exposure is not. `design/sweep.mjs` shows 5 and 6 make
  the current level unsolvable; that bounds it, but does not justify 8 specifically.
* *That the 3D form should have been rejected.* The measurement (0 spaceships from
  180 soups per rule) is real and is landed as evidence, but "no glider ecology"
  is an inference from it, not the measurement itself.

== NOT claimed

* *There is no game yet.* There is no renderer, no camera, no input handling and
  no playable bundle. Nothing in this repository can be played. The 3D layer,
  its inertness proof (C7) and bundle reproducibility (C10) are unlanded, and
  their claim numbers are reserved rather than quietly reused.
* *The level is not claimed to be good, or even finished* — see C6.
* *No claim about performance.* `shadowMap` is O(W·H·sensors·ray) and is intended
  to be recomputed on CA tick, not per frame. Nothing measures that yet.
* *No claim that the solver is a second implementation of the engine.* It is an
  approximation that orders the collision and radar tests differently for speed.
  It may only propose; `verify-witness.mjs` flying the route through the real
  `missionStep()` is what makes C5 a claim. If the two ever disagree, the solver
  is wrong.
* *No claim about `spike/`.* Those two files are provenance, landed verbatim.
  They are not built, not linted, not imported, and their seven recorded defects
  are not fixed in place.
* *No security claim beyond the obvious.* This is a dependency-free static page
  with no network access and no user data.

== Deviations

* *C6 is red and CI does not gate on it.* Registered here rather than silently
  excluded. It runs on every push and reports.
* *`spike/` is exempt from line-ending normalisation* (`.gitattributes`), because
  `simulation.html` carries 162 CRLF terminators and `text=auto eol=lf` would
  rewrite them, breaking the published SHA-256 digests and the byte-identical
  claim with them.
