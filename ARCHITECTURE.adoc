== Why the world is 2D and the camera is 3D

Measured, not preferred: 3D Life has no glider ecology — 180 random soups per
rule on a 24³ torus produced *0 translating patterns* in Bays 5766 and in
4555 (claim C9, `src/verify-3d-rule-search.mjs`, the planning probe landed as
a permanent test). A coupled 3D CA game would have no player. So the world
stays literally Conway, and the three dimensions move to the *camera*: a
chase view whose flight model is adapted from `spike/simulation.html` — the
un-regressed prototype, with the PID controller and elevon mixer intact — and
which banks into the glider's turns. See `spike/README.md` for the lineage and
the seven measured defects of the other prototype; defects 4, 5 and 7 (axis
disagreement, sign-broken collision, vanishing aircraft) are fixed in
`src/render3d.mjs` by construction, and defects 1–3 are the reason the
substrate is not 3D.

== The inertness rule (C7)

`render3d.mjs` is a *pure function of its inputs*. It may read mission
state; it must not write it, and it cannot smuggle in nondeterminism because
it has no clock and no randomness to smuggle (C7 greps for the same banned
primitives C8 bans from the simulation). The proof is not the grep, though:
`verify-renderer.mjs` flies the witness twice — once headless, once invoking
the full camera-and-draw-list path every generation — and requires every
generation's world to hash identically and the outcome to match. It ships
with a canary: a deliberately mutating renderer must be caught by the same
harness.

`ui.js` sits above that line and is _presentation only_: it carries no
gameplay truth, and deleting it leaves every verification claim intact. The
frame clock lives there — legitimate, because C7 proves the layer below it
cannot influence the mission — and CA ticks are accumulator-driven while
rendering runs per frame. `shadowMap` is recomputed *on CA tick only*, per
the cost note in `src/radar.mjs`.

== The bundle (C10)

`src/build.mjs` concatenates `kernel/engine.mjs {plus} radar.mjs {plus} mission.mjs {plus} level.mjs {plus} render3d.mjs {plus} ui.js` with imports/exports stripped, inlines the
CSS, and emits a single self-contained `f117a-stealth-glider.html` — no
build tooling, no runtime modules, runs from a `file://` URL. Two checks run
at build time: `node --check` on the exact shipped script, and a smoke flight
of the committed witness through the _transformed_ core (must LAND, trace 0,
never locked). `just test` and CI then require the committed bundle to be
byte-identical to a fresh rebuild.
